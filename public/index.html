How this works. Bulk Swap never holds your funds or keys. It builds swap transactions using Jupiter's routing, and your wallet signs them. Before you approve, check the balance changes your wallet shows.

Risks. Swaps are final. Dust tokens often have little liquidity, and unverified tokens may be scams. Swaps that would lose more than your loss limit are skipped, but prices can still move. Provided as-is, with no warranty. Use at your own risk.
