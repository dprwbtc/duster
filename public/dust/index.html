how it works

How it works

  1. 01

    connect

    Read-only. Spacedust looks at your token balances and prices. Nothing gets signed.

  2. 02

    pick your dust

    Everything under $2 is picked for you. Change the range, search, or untick anything. Up to 30 per run.

  3. 03

    approve once

    One wallet prompt. Each token gets its own transaction, so one bad route never takes the rest down with it.

fee

1% fee, bought and burned

fine print

Non-custodial. Spacedust never holds your funds or keys. It builds transactions with Jupiter routing, simulates every one, and your wallet signs them. Check the balance changes your wallet shows before you approve.

Risks. Swaps are final. Dust tokens often have little liquidity, and unverified tokens may be scams. Swaps that would lose more than your loss limit are skipped, but prices can still move. Provided as-is, with no warranty. Use at your own risk.

rent · no fee

get your rent back

Every token account locks about 0.002 SOL of rent, even after it’s empty. Spacedust closes the empty ones and sends that SOL back to your wallet, about 25 accounts per transaction, all in one prompt. Spacedust adds no fee. Dust under $1 can be burned and closed too, but only if you pick it.
